Workflows redefined.

Far beyond a basic launcher, Dock Float features signature killer capabilities designed to push your productivity to the absolute limit.

Group: Extreme simplicity.
Everything in one icon.

No matter how many tools you juggle, your screen should stay pristine. Group any related apps, files, or folders into a single, beautifully unified icon on your Dock based on your own logic. Click the Group, and everything you tucked away instantly unfolds.

[ Group Feature Large Mockup / Video View ]

Flow: Connect the context.
Unbroken focus.

Tired of digging for the same files every time you launch an app? Flow solves this intelligently. Pre-link essential files and folders to a specific program. The moment you launch that program, your predefined toolkit seamlessly glides in. No searching. Just unbroken focus.

[ Flow Context-Aware Large Mockup / Video View ]

Dock Float Remote:
The ultimate wireless control center on your desk.

Extend your macOS command center directly into your mobile ecosystem. Open the Dock Float Remote app on your iPhone or iPad. Every Dock on your Mac syncs in real time. Launch apps, instantly open documents, and map Mission Control or Screen Capture to intuitive Action Buttons. Command your Mac wirelessly.

[ Mac & iPad Remote Sync Mockup / YouTube Video View ]

Shelf: Shake to open.
Toss to store.

Copied text, a quick screenshot, a file you’ll need in exactly two minutes. Stop littering your desktop. Just give your mouse a quick shake. The Shelf quietly wakes up on your screen. Toss anything into this temporary holding space and pull it out whenever you need it.

Dock Float Remote
The ultimate wireless control center on your desk.

Extend your macOS command center directly into your mobile ecosystem. Open the Dock Float Remote app on your iPhone or iPad. Every Dock on your Mac syncs in real time. A light tap on your device launches Mac apps or instantly opens documents.

We’ve also added customizable Action Button to supercharge your speed. Map Mission Control, Launchpad (or Apps in macOS 26+), and Screen Capture to intuitive buttons. Command your Mac wirelessly without learning new keyboard shortcuts. Turn on the 'Keep Your iPhone Awake' option (while plugged in), and you have a dedicated, always-on macro pad right at your fingertips.

Shelf Shake to open. Toss to store.

Copied text, a quick screenshot, a file you’ll need in exactly two minutes. Stop littering your desktop. Just give your mouse a quick shake. The Shelf quietly wakes up on your screen. Toss anything into this temporary holding space and pull it out whenever you need it. It’s your secret workspace, designed to ensure your focus never breaks.

Capture screenshots. Beautifully simple

All the essential screenshot features you need are built right in: Capture Area, Capture Previous Area, Capture Window, Capture Fullscreen, and Capture with Timer. Access them through the menu or use your preferred keyboard shortcuts for lightning-fast captures.

But here's where Deskeen truly shines—we've hidden some remarkable features that will transform how you capture screens.

Freeform and Precision capture modes, available exclusively in Capture Area mode.

Freeform mode gives you complete freedom to select any area you want with intuitive ease, while Precision mode delivers pixel-perfect accuracy for those moments when every detail matters. Freeform mode has an incredible hidden gem: hold the Control key while dragging to activate Instant Screen Pin — this captures your selection without creating a file, perfect for quick reference. Press the Space key to instantly capture any window beneath your cursor.

Precision mode lives up to its name with arrow key controls that let you adjust your capture area pixel by pixel. Hold Shift to move in convenient 10-pixel increments. And yes, you get the same powerful Instant Screen Pin feature—just one click away.

Could essential features get any simpler or more robust? Ready to discover what Deskeen can really do?

Record Any Screen. Any Window.

Recording your screen is as effortless as taking a screenshot. Capture any area you want or record specific windows with precision and ease. Choose your preferred encoder from H.264, HEVC, fine-tune video quality with frame rates of 15, 24, 25, 30, or 60 FPS, and select your ideal audio format from AAC, ALAC, or FLAC.

Every recording captures all system audio from your Mac plus microphone input, giving you complete audio control. When you're done, easily export your videos as Animated GIFs or extract Audio files—whatever your project needs.

Introducing Presenter Overlay—a game-changing feature that uses your Mac's connected camera to show you alongside your screen recording. Position your overlay anywhere you want for the perfect presentation setup.

We've also included handy utilities like cursor highlighting and click effects to make your recordings more engaging and professional. Combine these with Deskeen 2's powerful Screen Annotation feature, and the possibilities are truly limitless.

Screen and window recording with Deskeen is beautifully simple and incredibly powerful.

Instant Screen Pin

Instant Screen Pin is a revolutionary feature built on our Screen Pin foundation. Traditionally, capturing means creating files, then viewing those files—a seemingly natural process that actually involves many unnecessary steps.

But what if you just want to capture and view without saving? That's exactly what Instant Screen Pin solves.

You decide whether to save after seeing the result—giving you complete control over your workflow. Instant Screen Pin provides nearly all the powerful features of regular Screen Pin. In Freeform mode, simply hold Control while dragging. In Precision mode, just click the Instant Screen Pin icon in the selection guide UI. Simple. Powerful. Revolutionary.

Scrolling Capture with Auto-Scroll

Turn endless web pages into single, complete images!

Scrolling Capture automatically stitches together long web pages or documents from top to bottom, creating one seamless image. No more multiple screenshots—just scroll and let Deskeen capture everything continuously.

  • Captures all scrolling content without missing anything (scroll slowly for optimal accuracy)
  • Supports auto-scroll for accurate and convenient scrolling capture.
  • High-capacity capture: Up to 20,000 pixels supported, with helpful warnings for larger files (Apple Silicon recommended)
  • Easy identification: Special icons in Capture History distinguish scrolling captures from regular screenshots

One click preserves entire long-form content perfectly—incredibly convenient!

Auto Capture with Page Capture

Automatically capture multiple screens in sequence!

Page Capture automatically captures slide presentations, long web pages, or any multi-screen content in sequence. Set it up once, and Deskeen handles the page-turning and capturing automatically. Perfect for capturing every slide in HTML presentations or Keynote slides, creating video summaries by capturing YouTube frames at intervals, systematically capturing web page sections while scrolling, or documenting every screen in multi-page applications. The process is amazingly simple: specify your target application, set the navigation key (spacebar, arrow keys, etc.), define the number of images and wait time, then hit capture—Deskeen handles everything else automatically. Works seamlessly whether content flows horizontally (slides) or vertically (scrolling pages).

Comprehensive Options Include:

  • Application selection for targeted capture
  • Custom shortcut key configuration for page navigation
  • Flexible image count specification
  • Multiple output formats: regular images, Animated GIF, PDF
  • Custom folder naming for organized storage
  • Adjustable capture intervals plus dedicated YouTube mode

Set it once, and completely automate repetitive capture tasks—absolutely convenient!
